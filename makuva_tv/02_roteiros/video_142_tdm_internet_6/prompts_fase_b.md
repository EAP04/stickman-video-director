# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 142: TDM e Internet (6)

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — Tento conectar (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with home office with Wi-Fi router, modem and computer desk. Stick figure holds phone, tries to connect to internet, signal bars drop, nothing loads, figure looks annoyed. Small yellow signal accent visible. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — Sem sinal (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure holding phone on white background with home office with Wi-Fi router, modem and computer desk. Phone shows no signal, webpage does not load, figure looks frustrated, tries again. White stick figure, black stick limbs, smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Desligo o roteador (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure turns off the router, puts phone down, looks defeated. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, vermelho
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de celular/notificação (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
