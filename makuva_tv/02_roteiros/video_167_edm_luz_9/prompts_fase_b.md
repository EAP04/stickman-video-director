# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 167: EDM e Luz (9)

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — A luz voltou (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with bedroom with bed, bedside lamp and window with curtains. Stick figure is inside house, power comes back on, light turns on, figure is happy. Small yellow lightbulb accent visible. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — A luz acabou outra vez (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure inside house on white background with bedroom with bed, bedside lamp and window with curtains. Light goes out again shortly after coming back, room is dark, figure looks frustrated. White stick figure, black stick limbs, smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Espero a luz (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure stays in dark house waiting for power to come back again, looks patient but tired. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, vermelho
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de luz (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
