# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 10: Hospital e Saúde

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — Chegar cedo, tirar senha (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with clinic examination room with bed, medical equipment and cabinets. Stick figure sits in hospital waiting room holding queue ticket, looks at door, waits patiently. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — Senha não avança (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure sitting in waiting room on white background with clinic examination room with bed, medical equipment and cabinets. Figure looks at wristwatch repeatedly, looks at display board that does not change, looks exhausted. White stick figure, black stick limbs, small red number accent on board visible. Smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Chama outro número, vai embora (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure stands up as door opens and another number is called, shrugs, walks out of hospital holding ticket. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, vermelho
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de porta abrindo ou painel eletrônico (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
