# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 166: Pão e Mercado (9)

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — Vou ao mercado (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with bakery counter with bread display, shelves and shop counter. Stick figure runs to market to buy bread, arrives at closed door, market is shut. Small yellow bread accent visible. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — O mercado fechou (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure at market door on white background with bakery counter with bread display, shelves and shop counter. Door is closed with closed sign, figure looks at door disappointed, tries to open but nothing. White stick figure, black stick limbs, smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Compro amanhã (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure turns around, walks away from closed market empty handed, will buy bread tomorrow. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, vermelho
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de mercado (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
