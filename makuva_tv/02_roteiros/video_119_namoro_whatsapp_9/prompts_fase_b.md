# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 119: Namoro e WhatsApp (9)

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — Espero mensagem (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with bedroom with bed, phone on nightstand and window. Stick figure holds phone, waits for important WhatsApp message, phone does not ring, is on silent mode, figure looks confused. Small red silent mode accent visible. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — Não toca (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure holding phone on white background with bedroom with bed, phone on nightstand and window. Figure checks phone again, no message arrived, phone stayed silent the whole time, looks frustrated. White stick figure, black stick limbs, smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Tiro silencioso (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure turns off silent mode on phone, puts phone away, walks away looking relieved. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, vermelho
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de WhatsApp (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
