# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 159: Escola e Professores (3)

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — Estou na aula (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with classroom with blackboard, desks and teacher desk. Stick figure sits in school classroom during lesson, teacher calls another student name instead of his, figure looks embarrassed. Small yellow desk accent visible. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — O professor errou (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure in classroom on white background with classroom with blackboard, desks and teacher desk. Teacher points and says wrong name, figure stays silent looking awkward, classmates look at him. White stick figure, black stick limbs, smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Não respondo (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure stays quiet in class after wrong name, looks down embarrassed, does not answer the teacher. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, vermelho
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de escola (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
