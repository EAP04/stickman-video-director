# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 153: Banco e Filas (8)

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — Vou sacar dinheiro (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with bank interior with queue area, service counter and waiting chairs. Stick figure walks into bank holding card, approaches ATM, ATM is broken with out of order sign, figure looks disappointed. Small yellow card accent visible. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — O caixa está quebrado (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure at ATM on white background with bank interior with queue area, service counter and waiting chairs. ATM screen is dark, machine does not work, figure tries again, nothing happens, looks frustrated. White stick figure, black stick limbs, smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Vou outro banco (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure turns around, leaves bank holding card, walks away looking defeated. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, vermelho
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de banco (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
