# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 69: Polícia e Trânsito (3)

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — O farol está queimado (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with police checkpoint with officer uniform, traffic sign and street. Stick figure rides motorcycle at night, headlight does not turn on, figure looks at dashboard worried. Small yellow headlight accent visible. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — A polícia pede documentos (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure on motorcycle on white background with police checkpoint with officer uniform, traffic sign and street. Police officer waves to stop motorcycle because headlight is off, asks for driver's license, figure looks nervous. White stick figure, black stick limbs, smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Tomei multa (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure holds ticket from police officer, looks annoyed, pushes motorcycle forward walking beside it. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, amarelo
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de moto ou sirene (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
