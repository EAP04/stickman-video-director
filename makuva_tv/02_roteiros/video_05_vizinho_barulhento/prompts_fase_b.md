# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 5: Vizinho Barulhento

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — Tentar dormir, som começa (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with apartment hallway with door, neighbor sounds and building interior. Stick figure lies in bed, pulls blanket over head, covers ears, looks annoyed as loud music starts next door. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — Bater na parede, volume sobe (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure sits up in bed on white background with apartment hallway with door, neighbor sounds and building interior. Figure bangs on wall with fist, looks at wall, music note or speaker icon appears on other side of wall getting louder. White stick figure, black stick limbs, small red sound accent. Smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Amanheceu, sem dormir (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure sits on edge of bed looking exhausted with dark circles under eyes. Window shows sunrise with yellow light. Figure looks at camera with tired defeated expression. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, yellow sunrise accent. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, vermelho, amarelo
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de música abafada ou batida na parede (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
