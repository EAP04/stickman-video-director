# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 161: Kombi e Chapas (9)

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — Espero o kombi (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with minibus taxi interior with seats, handrails and passengers. Stick figure stands at kombi stop with hand raised, kombi arrives and keeps going without stopping, figure watches it pass. Small yellow kombi accent visible. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — O kombi não para (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure on roadside on white background with minibus taxi interior with seats, handrails and passengers. Kombi drives away in distance, figure keeps hand raised then lowers it looking disappointed. White stick figure, black stick limbs, smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Espero o outro (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure waits alone by the roadside again for the next kombi, hand raised, looks patient. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, vermelho
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de kombi (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
