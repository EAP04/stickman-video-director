# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 122: Celular e Rede (9)

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — Desligou (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with living room with sofa, phone in hand and window. Stick figure holds phone, phone suddenly turns off by itself, figure looks shocked. Small red empty battery accent visible. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — Bateria acabou (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure holding dead phone on white background with living room with sofa, phone in hand and window. Figure tries to turn phone on, nothing happens, battery is dead, looks frustrated. White stick figure, black stick limbs, smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Carrego (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure plugs phone into charger, sits down, waits for battery to charge. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, vermelho
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de celular (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
