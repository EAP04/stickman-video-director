# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 145: Xiga/Taxi (7)

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — Chamo o xiga (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with taxi interior with driver seat, dashboard and street view. Stick figure stands by roadside waving hand, yellow xiga taxi approaches, taxi drives past without stopping. Small yellow taxi accent visible. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — Passou direto (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure on roadside on white background with taxi interior with driver seat, dashboard and street view. Second xiga passes by without stopping, figure keeps arm raised looking disappointed. White stick figure, black stick limbs, smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Vou a pé (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure turns around, puts arm down, walks away down the road alone. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, vermelho
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de estrada/buzina (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
