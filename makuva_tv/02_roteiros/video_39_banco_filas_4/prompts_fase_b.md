# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 39: Banco e Filas (4)

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — Minha vez passou (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with bank interior with queue area, service counter and waiting chairs. Stick figure stands in bank line holding ticket, looks away for a moment, number changes, figure looks annoyed. Small red number accent on board visible. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — O próximo já entrou (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure waiting in bank line on white background with bank interior with queue area, service counter and waiting chairs. Display board shows next number called, figure looks at board, realizes turn was missed. White stick figure, black stick limbs, smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Saio da fila, volta amanhã (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure turns around, walks out of bank holding ticket, decides to come back another day. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, vermelho
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de banco ou painel (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
