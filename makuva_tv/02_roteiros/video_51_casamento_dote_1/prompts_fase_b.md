# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 51: Casamento e Dote (1)

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — A lista do dote (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with wedding venue with chairs, decorations and celebration setup. Stick figure sits at table with father-in-law figure, paper list on table, first dowry item mentioned. Small yellow paper accent visible. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — A lista cresce (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure at table on white background with wedding venue with chairs, decorations and celebration setup. List gets longer with more items added, father-in-law looks at list, figure looks worried. White stick figure, black stick limbs, smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Vou pensar nisso (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure stands up from table, says needs to think, walks away from negotiation. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, amarelo
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de papel/caneta (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
