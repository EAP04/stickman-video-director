# Prompts Fase B — Vídeo 121: Água e Chafariz (9)

Copie e cole cada prompt separadamente na ferramenta de geração de vídeo (Google Flow, Runway, Luma ou Kling). Cada prompt gera um clipe de ~10 segundos.

## Clipe 1 — A água jorra (0–10s)

```
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure on white background with outdoor street with water fountain, pavement and neighborhood. Stick figure arrives at water pump, water flows strongly, figure realizes bucket is at home, looks disappointed. Small red water jet accent visible. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 2 — Esqueci o balde (10–20s)

```
Same minimalist 2D animated stick figure at water pump on white background with outdoor street with water fountain, pavement and neighborhood. Looks at empty hands, no bucket to collect water, water keeps flowing, looks frustrated. White stick figure, black stick limbs, smooth 2D animation. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Clipe 3 — Vou buscar (20–30s)

```
Same stick figure turns around from water pump, walks home to get bucket. Hollow circular head, black stick limbs, uniform medium line weight. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style. 9:16 vertical aspect ratio, 10 seconds duration. No speech bubbles, no text, no dialogue boxes.
```

## Regras de geração (não alterar)
- Proporção: 9:16 vertical
- Duração: ~10 segundos por clipe
- Nenhum texto, número ou interface na cena
- Personagem consistente (mesma cabeça, mesmos membros, mesma linha)
- Nenhum membro ocioso por mais de 2–3 segundos
- Cores apenas com nomes comuns: branco, preto, vermelho
- Não usar hex, RGB ou Pantone

## Após gerar
Baixe os 3 clipes (MP4) e monte no CapCut, Premiere ou editor que preferir. Adicione:
1. Narração em português de Moçambique
2. Legendas sincronizadas
3. Efeito sonoro de água (opcional)
4. Thumbnail com cores verde, vermelho, amarelo
